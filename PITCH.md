# A new website for Black & Gold East Bay

**The goal: more people in Hayward finding the shop on Google and
booking a chair from their phone.**

## What's holding the current site back

When someone searches "barber Hayward" or "fade near me", Google decides
which shops to show partly by reading their websites. Right now the
site gives Google very little to go on:

- **No description and no main heading on any page.** The browser tab
  just says "BLACK & GOLD EAST BAY", with no "barber" or "Hayward".
- **The business details Google reads are blank.** The hidden info
  block has an empty address and empty hours.
- **The Appointments page still shows Squarespace's sample text**
  ("It all begins with an idea…"), and Google can find it.
- **No prices or times**, and no map, FAQ or reviews.
- **The photos have no descriptions**, so Google can't tell what they
  show, and neither can visitors who use screen readers.
- **Small things that look unfinished:** an empty shopping cart icon,
  the "APPOINMENT" typo, hours that list Monday twice, and a link
  preview that's a screenshot.
- **It's slow on phones.** The page is nearly 1 MB, mostly Squarespace
  code and the background video.

## What the new site fixes

- **Built to show up in local searches.** Every page has a clear title
  and description with "barber" and "Hayward". There's a page for each
  service (haircuts and fades, beard trims, hot towel shaves), plus a
  Location page and an FAQ. The business details Google reads are
  filled in correctly.
- **Booking in one tap.** "Book now" is always on screen, including a
  bar that stays at the bottom of the phone. Each barber has a
  "Book with …" button that opens their own Blismo calendar.
- **Blismo stays exactly as it is.** Bookings, availability and each
  barber's schedule stay in Blismo. Nothing changes for the barbers.
- **Instagram messages.** Each barber can get a "Message on Instagram"
  button for questions, once we confirm their handle.
- **Same black and gold, sharpened.** The look is inspired by
  gold-leaf barbershop window lettering, and it's clean and easy to
  read.
- **Fast.** The home page is about 12 times lighter than today's.
- **Works for everyone.** It's built to accessibility standards
  (WCAG 2.1 AA), which also protects the shop from ADA complaints.
- **Easy to update.** Hours, prices and barbers all live in one simple
  file.

| **New site** (computer) | **100** | **100** | **100** | **100** |

The current site's main content takes about **8 seconds** to show up on
a phone; the new one takes about **1.4 seconds**. (Tested 2026-10-13.
The new site was tested before going live, so we'll re-check both on
Google's PageSpeed Insights once it's online.)

The current site's 92 SEO score sounds good, but that test only checks
the basics. It doesn't notice the blank business info, the missing
headings or the sample text, which are what actually matter for local
search.

## What we need from you

Nothing ships with a guess. These are placeholders on the new site
until you confirm them:

**Shop details**
1. Your real hours for each day.
2. Is the name, address and phone exactly as on your Google listing?
3. Prices and how long each service takes. Any services to add (kids'
   cuts, line-ups, designs)?
4. Your Google review link (from your Google Business Profile).
5. The map pin location from your Google listing.
6. Where clients should park, and any landmarks for directions.
7. Is "over three decades of combined experience" still right?
8. Is "Downtown Hayward" how you'd describe the location?

**Barbers**
9. Each barber's Instagram handle. (Jamie, Francis, Mel, Joe and Mark
   are all placeholders right now.)
10. Each barber's photo, a short bio and their specialty, with their
    OK to use them.
11. Please tap each barber's "Book with" button once to confirm it
    opens the right calendar.

**Policies (for the FAQ)**
12. Walk-ins: yes or no, and when?
13. Kids' cuts: offered? What ages?
14. Payment methods and tipping.
15. Cancellation and no-show policy.

**Photos and reviews**
16. Cut photos we can use for the gallery, and who took them.
17. Any reviews you're happy for us to quote on the site.
18. A high-quality copy of your logo.

**Accounts and choices**
19. Who owns the domain blackandgoldeastbay.com, and where is it
    registered?
20. Who manages your Google Business Profile?
21. Do you want to keep the background video?
22. Squarespace currently stops AI assistants (like ChatGPT) from
    reading your site. Keep that, or let them recommend you?
23. After launch, who updates hours when they change?
